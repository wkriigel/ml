# Minor Light

A solo dice game, in playtesting.

## Play


Open `index.html` in a web browser (double-click it). There is nothing to install. Your campaign
saves itself in the browser as you play.

## Phone layout



For portrait play, choose **Phone layout** on the title screen. The board stays
in view; swipe the hands or use their edge arrows to see more dice and cards.
Tap a hand card to read it before playing. **Inspect** shows a die's faces and
related rules, and **Read** opens narration that does not fit its preview.
Full readers can scroll. **Disable guide** silences automatic advice; it remains
available to read from Menu. Choose **Original layout** in Menu to switch back.

## The bar at the bottom

On the original layout, hover over a button to see its name.

- **Menu** opens the campaign controls. **Load Game** is on the title screen.
- **Download Campaign Data**, at the far right in the original layout, downloads
  a record of your play for the designer. In phone mode, use **Export campaign** in Menu.
  Send that file when you are asked for it. It is not a save; use the Menu for that.
