# Minor Light

A solo dice game, in playtesting.

## Play


Open `index.html` in a web browser (double-click it). There is nothing to install. Your campaign
saves itself in the browser as you play.

## The bar at the bottom

Hover over any button to see its name.

- **Menu** continues, saves your game to a file, loads a saved game, or starts a new campaign.
- **Download Campaign Data**, at the far right, downloads a record of your play for the designer.
  Send that file when you are asked for it. It is not a save; use the Menu for that.
